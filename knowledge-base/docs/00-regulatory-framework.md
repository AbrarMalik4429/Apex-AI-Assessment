# CHI regulatory framework and statutory benefits (Saudi Arabia)

## Regulatory framework

- The regulator was the Council of Cooperative Health Insurance (CCHI), renamed Council of Health Insurance (CHI) in 2022. Since 4 March 2024, insurer licensing and complaints moved to the Insurance Authority; CHI keeps the benefits package and unified policy wording. `[REGULATORY]`
- Employers must provide CHI-compliant insurance for expatriate employees; valid cover is checked at visa/Iqama issuance and renewal. `[REGULATORY]`
- Insurers may not issue a policy with benefits below the Essential Benefits Package. `[REGULATORY]`
- Market "Class/Category A, B, C" (and VIP) are insurer package tiers, not official CHI classes. Roughly: A = broadest private network and highest limits; B = mid-range; C = basic/government-linked network. `[TYPICAL]`

## Standard exclusions across most compliant policies
- Cosmetic and elective procedures without medical justification `[TYPICAL]`
- Fertility treatment/IVF beyond diagnosis
- Experimental treatment and clinical trials
- Weight-loss surgery unless medically urgent (e.g. Bupa Family explicitly excludes sleeve gastrectomy)
- Non-emergency treatment outside the approved network without pre-approval
- Self-inflicted injuries
- Pre-existing conditions: waiting periods reported from roughly 9 to 24 months depending on insurer and contract `[VARIES]`

## Pre-authorization
Elective surgery, MRI/CT, non-emergency admission, maternity admission and specialised treatment normally need insurer pre-approval, usually requested by the provider. `[TYPICAL]`

## Cancellation & refunds
- Employer-mandated policies are generally not cancellable by the employee mid-term. `[TYPICAL]`
- Individual/non-mandatory policies can usually be cancelled by the policyholder; the insurer refunds the unused premium within a few working days of notice.
- Domestic-worker or similar individual policies are typically cancelled pro-rata on final exit.
- Mechanics differ by insurer: treat as `[VARIES]` for any specific policy.
