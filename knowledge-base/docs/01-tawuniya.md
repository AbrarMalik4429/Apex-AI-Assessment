# Tawuniya (The Company for Cooperative Insurance)

## Overview
Founded in 1986, Tawuniya is Saudi Arabia's oldest health insurer and, with Bupa Arabia, one of the two dominant medical insurers by premium share. `[TYPICAL]` Strong network presence in Riyadh, Jeddah and industrial cities (Jubail, Yanbu, Hail). `[TYPICAL]`

## Product lines
- **My Family** (individual/family): four tiers, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond.
- **Balsam** and **360 Health Insurance**: employer/corporate group plans (SME to enterprise). Per-tier figures not published; the CHI floor applies.
- Specialised: visit-visa, Hajj/Umrah, parents, domestic-worker health insurance.

## Qualifies
In- and out-patient care, surgery (as in-patient), maternity, capped dental/optical, physiotherapy, chronic care from inception, local ambulance. `[INSURER]`

## Does not qualify / limited
- Treatment outside the Kingdom, except emergency cover on Diamond (day-capped, co-insurance applies). `[INSURER]`
- Anything above sub-limits (dental, optical, maternity) is the member's cost.
- 20% deductible on essentially every claim on this line.

## Policy administration
- Cover starts 30 days after full annual premium payment. `[INSURER]`
- Mid-term additions limited to newborns and new spouses.
- Dependent age limits apply (males to 18; unmarried/divorced/widowed females to 60; adult enrolment up to 60).
