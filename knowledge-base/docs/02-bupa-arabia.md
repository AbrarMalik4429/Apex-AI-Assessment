# Bupa Arabia (Bupa Arabia for Cooperative Insurance)

## Overview
Founded 1997, HQ Jeddah, associate of the Bupa Group; with Tawuniya it holds roughly three-quarters of the medical market. Largest private hospital/clinic network, strongest in Riyadh, Jeddah and the Eastern Province. `[TYPICAL]`

## Family plan eligibility
Head of household applies for all members on the same Family Card; all must be covered. A member is excluded if they hold another active policy, live abroad, or are married / a male dependent over 25. `[INSURER]`
