# Al Rajhi Takaful (Al Rajhi Company for Cooperative Insurance)

## Overview
Established 2010, roughly 14% of health premium at last report `[TYPICAL]`. Operates on a Shariah-compliant Takaful (cooperative) model, licensed by SAMA. Patient experience of cover is similar to conventional insurance.

## Product lines
- **Unified Health Insurance** (employees and families; SME and corporate)
- Individual and family health plans
- Domestic-worker and visitor-visa medical insurance

Public material describes flexible benefit limits and provider choice, and says packages can be personalised, but the same package must then apply to the whole family `[INSURER]`. No named tier ladder with published limits was found.

## Policy administration notes
- Purchase of unified health policy verifies the owner via a mobile number registered in Absher. `[INSURER]`
- App supports one-time-passcode login and shows benefits, limits and exclusions to members. A real Saudi insurer already uses the OTP pattern planned for this assistant.
- Employers with 200+ lives can pay annual premium in monthly instalments via Al Rajhi Bank.

## Qualifies / does not qualify
Standard market exclusions apply (see the CHI regulatory framework document); no materially different exclusions are publicly advertised. `[TYPICAL]`
