# MedGulf (Mediterranean and Gulf Cooperative Insurance and Reinsurance Co.)

## Overview
Operating in Saudi Arabia since 1995, independent entity since 2006, listed 2007. Consistently third-largest health insurer (~11-13% of medical premium). `[TYPICAL]`

## Product lines
- **Group Health Insurance**: customised corporate cover (consultations, medications, labs, admissions, surgeries). `[INSURER]`
- **RIADI** (SME): categories by group size (e.g. 11-20 members), each with Basic and Advanced options. Employers choose room type, deductible ratios, network depth and add-ons.
- **Nabd** (SME): includes a medication-refill service for ongoing needs.
- **Salem**: programme for larger enterprises.

Which specific benefits Advanced adds over Basic is not published; do not guess.
