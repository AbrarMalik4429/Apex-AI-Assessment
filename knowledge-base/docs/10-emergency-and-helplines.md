# Saudi Arabia emergency numbers and health helplines

## Ambulance number in Saudi Arabia
The ambulance emergency number in Saudi Arabia is **997**. It is the dedicated line of the Saudi Red Crescent Authority. `[OFFICIAL]` The unified emergency number **911** also works and routes the caller to the right authority. `[OFFICIAL]`

## When to call an ambulance and the Asafny app
Call 997 straight away for life-threatening situations such as a suspected heart attack, stroke, choking, a major accident, severe bleeding or unconsciousness. `[TYPICAL]` The Saudi Red Crescent's Asafny app can be used to request medical help and follow the ambulance's location. `[TYPICAL]` Ambulance response times can vary by area. `[TYPICAL]` Never delay calling for a life-threatening emergency to look for information.

## Police, fire and traffic numbers
Police: **999** (911 also reaches police). `[OFFICIAL]` Civil Defense for fire, rescue, gas leaks and disasters: **998**. `[OFFICIAL]` Traffic police: **993**. `[TYPICAL]` The European-style 112 number may not work reliably in Saudi Arabia, so use the local numbers. `[TYPICAL]`

## Unified emergency number 911
911 is the unified security operations number. It is active in the Makkah, Riyadh and Eastern regions, with expansion to all provinces planned. `[OFFICIAL]` Some sources also list Madinah as covered, so coverage in a given city may differ. `[VARIES]` If 911 does not connect, dial the direct numbers: 997 ambulance, 999 police, 998 civil defense.

## 937 Ministry of Health call centre
**937** is the Ministry of Health's health call centre and it operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. `[OFFICIAL]` It provides medical consultation with doctors, guidance in poisoning cases, booking of primary-health-centre appointments, referrals, complaints and technical support for the Ministry's apps. `[OFFICIAL]` Service is offered in Arabic, English, French, Urdu, Indonesian, Farsi and Turkish, and from outside the Kingdom the number is 00966920005937. `[OFFICIAL]` 937 is a health advice line, not an ambulance dispatch: for a life-threatening emergency call 997 or 911.

## Poisoning emergencies
For suspected poisoning call **937** for guidance from Ministry of Health doctors. `[OFFICIAL]` A poison control line run from King Fahd Medical City in Riyadh is reported as 800 127 7000. `[TYPICAL]` If the person is unconscious, having seizures or struggling to breathe, call 997 or 911 immediately.

## Mental health support and crisis lines
The Ministry of Health psychological consultation line is **920033360**, staffed by mental health professionals. `[OFFICIAL]` Its published hours differ between pages (roughly 8:00 to 20:00, shorter on Saturdays), so confirm hours before relying on it. `[VARIES]` The 937 line answers 24 hours a day but is a general health line rather than a crisis service. `[OFFICIAL]` The NCMH "Qareboon" app offers counselling, and the National Family Safety Programme youth line is 800 122 2777 (about 8:00 to 22:00, Sunday to Thursday). `[TYPICAL]` For immediate danger to life, call 997 or 911.
Assistant rule: if a patient expresses thoughts of self-harm or suicide, do not continue with booking or general answers. Respond with care, give these numbers, and escalate to a human. `[DEMO_CONFIG]`

## Domestic violence and family safety
The domestic violence hotline is reported as **1919**. `[TYPICAL]` Verify before quoting, since sources are secondary. In immediate danger call 999, 911 or 997.

## Emergency treatment and ability to pay
Initial stabilising emergency treatment is broadly available to patients regardless of ability to pay, but continued or non-urgent care at public hospitals is not free for expatriates, who must hold health insurance. `[TYPICAL]` Ambulances cannot legally refuse to transport a patient to hospital. `[TYPICAL]` Carry your Iqama or national ID and your insurance card at all times. `[TYPICAL]`

## What the assistant does if a patient describes an emergency
If a message describes a possible emergency (chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding, stroke signs, loss of consciousness, poisoning, suicidal thoughts), the assistant does not start a booking. It tells the patient to call 997 or 911 immediately, then escalates to a human team member. While help is on the way it may share the matching standard steps from the first-aid guide (for example CPR or choking). It does not diagnose or advise on treatment beyond those steps. `[DEMO_CONFIG]`
